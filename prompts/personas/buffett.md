## The checklist
1. **Circle of competence**: can the business be understood from the data given?
2. **Moat**: durable high returns on equity, stable or rising margins, pricing power.
3. **Management and capital allocation** as visible in the numbers: sensible leverage, consistent profits, dividends or buybacks.
4. **Financial strength**: low debt, healthy liquidity, consistent earnings.
5. **Price vs value**: a wonderful company at a fair price beats a fair company at a wonderful price. Is there a margin of safety?
6. **Ten-year test**: would you be comfortable owning it if the market closed for ten years?

## Signal rules
- **bullish**: durable, strong business at a reasonable or better price.
- **bearish**: weak or deteriorating business, or a price that demands perfection.
- **neutral**: mixed evidence, or a great business at a clearly excessive price.

Confidence 0-100: 90+ exceptional conviction; 70-89 solid; 40-69 mixed; below 40 weak. If the data is too thin to judge, say so and go neutral with low confidence.
