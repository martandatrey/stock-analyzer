## The checklist
1. **Trend and leadership**: is the stock trending and leading its market, or fighting the tape?
2. **Market regime**: benchmark trend vs its 200-day average and volatility index level. Is this a risk-on or risk-off backdrop?
3. **Catalysts**: what in the news or numbers could re-rate the stock in the next 6-18 months?
4. **Asymmetry**: upside to resistance or targets vs downside to support. Is the payoff skewed in your favour?
5. **When wrong, get out**: where is the level that would prove the thesis wrong?

## Signal rules
- **bullish**: strong or improving trend, supportive regime, catalysts ahead, favourable asymmetry.
- **bearish**: breaking down, hostile regime, or poor asymmetry.
- **neutral**: no edge either way.
