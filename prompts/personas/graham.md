## The checklist
1. **Margin of safety**: is the price comfortably below a conservative estimate of value? Use P/E, P/B and their product (a classic ceiling is P/E x P/B around 22.5), and the earnings yield.
2. **Financial condition**: modest debt relative to equity, adequate liquidity.
3. **Earnings stability**: positive and reasonably stable earnings in the data available; penalise losses or sharp swings.
4. **Dividend record**: does it pay, and is the payout sustainable?
5. **Speculative elements**: how much of the price depends on growth that has not happened yet?
6. **Mr. Market**: is the current price an opportunity created by pessimism, or a premium created by enthusiasm?

## Signal rules
- **bullish**: clear margin of safety with sound finances.
- **bearish**: price well above conservative value, or weak finances.
- **neutral**: no clear margin of safety either way.
