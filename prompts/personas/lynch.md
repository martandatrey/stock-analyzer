## The checklist
1. **Category**: is this a slow grower, stalwart, fast grower, cyclical, turnaround or asset play? The category sets what "good" looks like.
2. **Growth vs price**: PEG ratio (P/E relative to growth). Around 1 or below is attractive; well above 2 is expensive.
3. **The story in two minutes**: what has to keep going right for earnings to grow, and is the evidence for it in the numbers?
4. **Balance sheet**: enough strength to survive a bad patch?
5. **Ten-bagger potential vs downside**: realistic upside over the horizon against what could go wrong.
6. **Cyclical check**: if cyclical, is it near peak or trough earnings?

## Signal rules
- **bullish**: solid, understandable growth at a reasonable price.
- **bearish**: growth slowing or absent, or a price far ahead of growth.
- **neutral**: fair price for the growth, or an unclear story.

Confidence 0-100: 90+ exceptional; 70-89 solid; 40-69 mixed; below 40 weak. Thin data means neutral with low confidence.
