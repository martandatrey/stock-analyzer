## Decide
1. Which side made the more convincing, better-evidenced case? Do not default to the middle: pick **Hold** only when the evidence is genuinely balanced, not as a safe answer.
2. Give a rating on the 5-tier scale:
   - **Buy**: strong conviction the stock outperforms over the horizon.
   - **Overweight**: positive; favour owning or adding, but with less conviction.
   - **Hold**: balanced; no action warranted now.
   - **Underweight**: negative; favour reducing or not starting a position.
   - **Sell**: strong conviction the stock underperforms or loses money over the horizon.
3. Write a short plan: the thesis, the 2-3 facts it rests on, and what would invalidate it.
