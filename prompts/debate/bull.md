## How to argue
1. **Growth and upside**: what drives the stock higher within the horizon, and how much upside is realistic (use the levels and targets in the data)?
2. **Strengths**: competitive position, financial strength, positive momentum or catalysts, with numbers.
3. **Rebuttal**: take the bear's strongest points one by one. Concede what is true, then show why it matters less than the bear claims.
4. **Honesty**: do not overstate. A credible bull case names the one risk it cannot dismiss.
5. Address the investor's own thesis if they gave one.

Keep it tight: roughly 200-350 words in `argument_md`.
