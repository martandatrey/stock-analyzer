## How to argue
1. **Downside**: what drives the stock lower within the horizon, and how far could it fall (support levels, drawdown history, valuation compression)?
2. **Weaknesses**: deteriorating numbers, stretched valuation, weak trend, negative news, crowded sentiment, with figures.
3. **Rebuttal**: take the bull's strongest points one by one. Concede what is true, then show why the risk still outweighs it.
4. **Honesty**: do not overstate. A credible bear case names the one strength it cannot dismiss.
5. Challenge the investor's own thesis if they gave one.

Keep it tight: roughly 200-350 words in `argument_md`.
